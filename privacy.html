Kulus Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 31, 2026

Kulus is a glucose tracking and care communication app. This policy explains what information the Kulus mobile apps and the Kulus service collect, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address, name, and phone number, used to create and sign in to your Kulus account.
  • Health information you enter: blood glucose and weight readings, the time you took them, optional comments and tags, and optional photos of your glucose meter.
  • Care circle contacts: the names and phone numbers of family, friends, or care providers you choose to add so they can receive alerts.

How your information is used

  • To sync your readings between your devices and the Kulus cloud.
  • To send SMS alerts about your readings to the care circle contacts you choose. You control who is in your care circle, and the Snack Pass option lets you record a reading without alerting anyone.
  • To show you trends, statistics, and reminders inside the app.

Where your information is stored

Your data is stored on Microsoft Azure infrastructure hosted in Canada and is sent between your device and our servers over encrypted connections (HTTPS). On your device, your sign-in token is stored encrypted.

Optional AI photo reading

If you turn on AI photo reading and provide your own OpenAI API key, photos you take of your meter are sent to OpenAI to read the number on the screen. This is off by default. If you leave it off, photos are processed on your device only.

Service providers

We use Microsoft Azure to operate the Kulus service and store account and health data. When you enable an SMS alert, we use Telnyx to deliver it. The recipient's phone number and the alert text, which can include your name, reading, time, and optional comment, are sent to Telnyx for delivery. If you enable AI photo reading, the meter photo is sent to OpenAI as described above. These providers process information only to provide the requested Kulus feature.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your information.
  • We do not use your information for advertising.
  • We do not disclose your health information except to operate the service as described in this policy and to the care circle contacts you choose.

Data retention and deletion

Your information is kept while your account is active. To delete your account and associated data, contact us at the address below and we will remove it.

Children

Kulus is intended for use by adults and by minors only under the direction of a parent, guardian, or care provider.

Not medical advice

Kulus is a tracking and communication tool. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult your healthcare provider about your diabetes care.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: admin@kulus.org

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
